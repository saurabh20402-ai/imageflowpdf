# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

ImageFlow is committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or perspective.

We are dedicated to creating a community where everyone feels safe, respected, and valued.

## Our Standards

In the ImageFlow community, we strive to:

- **Be respectful** — treat all contributors with kindness and professionalism
- **Be clear** — communicate thoughtfully, especially in written form where tone can be misunderstood
- **Be inclusive** — actively welcome perspectives different from your own
- **Be constructive** — offer feedback that helps improve the project, not criticism that tears people down
- **Be accountable** — take responsibility for your words and actions
- **Focus on ideas** — critique suggestions and approaches, never the people who make them
- **Assume good intent** — unless there's clear evidence otherwise, assume people are trying to help

## What We Don't Tolerate

This community has zero tolerance for:

- Harassment of any kind — including threats, intimidation, or sustained negative behavior
- Discrimination based on race, ethnicity, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, religion, or any other characteristic
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Deliberate intimidation or exclusion
- Sharing others' private information without consent
- Spam, self-promotion, or commercial advertising unrelated to ImageFlow
- Any form of abuse or hostility

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All GitHub repositories and discussions under the ImageFlow project
- Pull request comments and code reviews
- Issue discussions and bug reports
- Slack, Discord, or any other official ImageFlow communication channels
- Interactions at conferences, meetups, or events representing ImageFlow
- Any space where you represent ImageFlow or its community

## Reporting a Violation

If you witness or experience a violation of this Code of Conduct:

1. **Do not engage** — don't escalate conflict by responding to the violation publicly
2. **Report it** — email **thewebrisecompany@gmail.com** (our official contact email for all security and conduct reports until dedicated email addresses are created) with:
   - Description of what happened
   - When and where it occurred
   - Who was involved (if safe to share)
   - Any links or screenshots
   - What resolution you're seeking
3. **Stay safe** — if you feel unsafe, prioritize your safety over reporting

All reports will be treated with confidentiality. We will not share your identity without permission unless required by law.

## Our Response

When a violation is reported, the ImageFlow team will:

1. **Acknowledge** the report within 48 hours
2. **Investigate** carefully and fairly, gathering facts from all parties
3. **Communicate** the findings and resolution
4. **Take action** proportional to the violation — which may include:
   - A private conversation addressing the behavior
   - A temporary ban from participation
   - A permanent ban from the community
   - Removal of content
   - Referral to appropriate authorities if necessary

We will not disclose details of the process or outcome except as necessary.

## Appeals

If you believe a decision was unfair, you may request an appeal by emailing thewebrisecompany@gmail.com within 30 days of the decision. An independent reviewer will examine the appeal.

## For Maintainers

Maintainers are held to the same standard as contributors. If a maintainer violates this Code of Conduct:

- Contributors can report it using the same process
- Leadership will recuse themselves from decisions about the violation if they are close to the person involved
- Maintainers who violate the Code may lose their maintainer status

## Appreciation

Thank you for helping make ImageFlow a place where everyone can contribute with confidence and respect.
